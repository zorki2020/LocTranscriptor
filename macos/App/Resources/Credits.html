Local transcription for meetings on your Mac, with speaker labels. Recordings and transcripts never leave it.

Speech recognition: NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (CC-BY-4.0).
Speaker diarization: pyannote Community-1 (CC-BY-4.0).
CoreML conversions and FluidAudio (Apache-2.0) by FluidInference.
Python, FastAPI, Starlette, Uvicorn, Jinja2, Pydantic, htmx and others under their own licenses.
